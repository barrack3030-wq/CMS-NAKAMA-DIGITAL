The Concept

An awakening of the senses.

NOIR is an exploration of contrast. We strip away the visual noise of the outside world to focus entirely on texture, aroma, and profound flavor profiles. In our dimly lit dining room, every dish is illuminated like a masterpiece.

A 12-course immersive tasting journey that redefines modern European gastronomy through the lens of seasonal, hyper-local ingredients.

Executive Chef

Julian Vance.

With over two decades in the kitchens of Paris, Tokyo, and New York, Chef Vance brings a relentless pursuit of perfection to NOIR. His philosophy is rooted in the belief that luxury is found in the purity of ingredients, manipulated with exact precision.
