01 — The Bread

Sourdough & Fire

Our bread is a living thing. Fermented for 48 hours and baked in a wood-fired hearth to achieve the perfect blistered crust and open crumb.

02 — The Process

Guided by touch

We don't rely on timers. We watch the dough, feel the humidity in the air, and listen to the crackle of the crust. It is an intuitive dance with nature.
